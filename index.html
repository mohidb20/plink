Plink

A diary of the films you've seen, and an atlas of cinema.

Plink is a free, non-commercial personal portfolio project. Log, rate and list films like a film diary, then explore an interactive map where films are grouped into regions by similarity, with the films you've watched lit up on the map.

It has no ads, no paid features and no sale of data.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Film data and images are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB).
